About College

D. R. Mane Mahavidyalaya, Kagal was established in 1984. The college provides higher education and opportunities for students to develop their academic and professional skills.

The college offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in different disciplines and is affiliated with Shivaji University, Kolhapur.
